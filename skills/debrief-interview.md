# Skill: Debrief an Interview

## When to use
Within 1-2 hours after every interview, while it's still fresh.

## Steps

1. **Capture what was asked**
   - List every question you remember, in order if possible
   - Note which ones caught you off guard

2. **Evaluate your answers**
   - Which answers were strong? What made them work?
   - Which answers were weak or incomplete? What was missing?
   - Did you use data and specifics, or stay too vague?

3. **Note signals from the interviewer**
   - Did they seem engaged or checked out?
   - Did they push back on anything? What specifically?
   - Any hints about what the team is prioritizing or struggling with?

4. **Identify gaps to close**
   - What would you answer differently if asked again?
   - Is there a topic or type of question you need to prepare better for?

5. **Next steps**
   - What did they say about timeline and process?
   - Do you need to send a follow-up note? (Send within 24 hours if yes)
   - Is there anything they asked you to send or follow up on?

## Output
Save as `interviews/[company]-[round]-debrief.md`

Include: date, interviewer name/role, questions asked, your self-assessment, and next steps.
