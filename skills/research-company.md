# Skill: Research a Company Before Applying or Interviewing

## When to use
Before tailoring a resume, writing outreach, or preparing for an interview.

## Steps

1. **Understand the business**
   - What does the company do and how does it make money?
   - What stage is it at (early startup, growth, public, enterprise)?
   - Who are their customers?

2. **Understand the product or platform**
   - What are their core products?
   - What problems are they solving?
   - Where are they investing (recent launches, acquisitions, job postings)?

3. **Understand the team and culture**
   - Who is the hiring manager and what is their background?
   - What do employees say (Glassdoor, Blind, LinkedIn)?
   - What values or operating principles do they publish?

4. **Find connection points**
   - Where does your background directly map to their problems?
   - Have they had issues you've solved before (supply chain, platform scaling, AI tooling)?
   - Do you know anyone there?

5. **Note recent news**
   - Funding rounds, product launches, layoffs, leadership changes
   - This shows up in interviews and outreach — use it to sound informed

## Output
A short notes file saved as `applications/[company]-research.md` or inline in the application record.
