# Skill: Write Cold Outreach

## When to use
When reaching out to someone at a target company — recruiter, hiring manager, or potential referral — without a prior relationship.

## Principles
- Be specific, not flattering. No "I love what you're doing at X."
- Make it easy to say yes. One clear ask per message.
- Keep it short. 4-6 sentences max.

## Steps

1. **Identify the right person**
   - Hiring manager for the role (preferred over recruiter for PM roles)
   - Someone in a similar role who can give a referral or warm intro
   - Find via LinkedIn, company website, or mutual connections

2. **Find a genuine connection point**
   - Shared background, school, or company
   - Something specific about their work or the company's product
   - A problem you've both worked on

3. **Draft the message using this structure:**
   - Line 1: Who you are in one sentence (current role, relevant background)
   - Line 2: Why you're reaching out to them specifically (not generic)
   - Line 3: What you've done that's relevant to their work
   - Line 4: The ask — specific and low-friction (a 15-min call, a referral, a question)

4. **Review before sending:**
   - Is the ask clear and easy to act on?
   - Does anything sound like flattery or filler? Cut it.
   - Would you respond to this message if you received it?

5. **Save the draft** as `outreach/[company]-[person].md` before sending.

6. **Follow up once** if no response after 7 days. Don't follow up more than once.

## Output
`outreach/[company]-[person].md`
